# A brief that survives construction repairs

Write a short `brief.md` or JSON equivalent before detailed construction. The design belongs to the request; references and tooling help realize it. Scale the brief to the task.

- Subject, story and intended scale.
- Major masses, silhouette and proportions; useful references and what you learned from them.
- Palette roles, main focal feature, supporting features, and quiet versus detailed areas.
- Modules, approximate envelopes, reserved openings, real attachment interfaces and build order.
- Features and relationships to preserve during construction repairs.

Use a small feature table. These are examples of concrete requirements, not mandatory features for every model:

| Feature | Visual/function intent | Spatial requirement | Final evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Porch | Shelters the entrance; subordinate to the roof | Canopy spans the doorway and intended depth; underside clears door height | Front/side view and verified supports |
| Chimney | Masonry shaft in proportion to the house | Continues to support through an opening in the roof; no brick resting on a smooth peak | Roof detail and support view |
| Dormer | Small secondary roof mass | Replaces roof cells in its footprint; window sits in the attic | Front and roof-off view |
| Steering | Wheels turn with the chosen stance | Linkage has clearance; wheelbase/track preserved | Studied interfaces and stated motion-check coverage |

Render the first composition before chasing every part number. Identify what the images reveal and revise the design. During engineering, update the evidence column with image/report paths and specific observations. A module name or a graph edge alone does not prove the feature has its intended size, position or function.

When a repair would weaken a feature, explore alternative supports, part choices or local layouts. Record any necessary change, why it was needed, and how the resulting design still meets the request. Do not quietly change the brief to match an accidental simplification. At delivery, list unmet features explicitly.

These are authoring and review records; no automatic beauty score or natural-language requirement verifier is implied.
