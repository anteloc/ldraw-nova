# Visual audit: submodel atlas

Planning evidence only. No tracked knowledge-base files or model geometry were changed.

**Coverage:** 30 packages, all 96 packaged model sections (30 roots + 66 nested children); 31 original-source boards and extracted manual images. Source and image hashes checked. Fresh xray views added where an initial view hid the relevant face or child. Embedded library DAT primitives are dependencies, not independently curated model examples.

**Limits:** these observations verify visible role, form and parent context. They do not certify fit, load, collision clearance or motion. Existing failed diagnostics remain failed. Other collections are not covered by this batch.

## Findings that change the migration plan

- Reconcile manual descriptions, catalog lessons, package guides, source-derived metadata and retrieval summaries. A corrected guide does not repair contradictory nested metadata.
- Christmas tree: two different foliage panels each repeated twice; remove four-identical-faces and tier descriptions.
- Buckbeak Step 34: rear head panel, not display base.
- Micro Tantive IV: replace tower-derived labels in all six children, including engines called sensors and stand called viewport.
- Helmet mouth module: a rail plate does not establish a sliding mechanism; a mudguard does not make the child vehicle bodywork.
- Keep the four known failing sources as repair/inspiration candidates until scoped diagnostics are resolved.
