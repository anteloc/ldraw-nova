# SNOT visual audit: parrot, cat and Halcyon

Planning evidence, 2026-11-01. No tracked examples or documentation changed.

Reviewed 3 packages, all 30 model sections (3 roots and 27 children), alone from two views and in each actual parent. All 16 contact sheets were opened. Fresh step views exposed the parrot head core, cat ear attachments and Halcyon side-stud course. All 57 initial render jobs and supplementary renders completed successfully. Source hashes remain unchanged. Standalone parrot head, cat head and cat leg have identical type-1 geometry to their included sections; evidence is reused rather than treating copies as new lessons.

| Candidate | Visual finding | Planned treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Parrot whole | Distinct head, body, paired articulated wings, paired tail segments and foliage perch match the composition described. | Retain animal composition; preserve actual handed parts and articulation limits. |
| Parrot head | Fresh intermediate steps expose a multi-sided stud core and perpendicular eye/beak supports; completed views establish the face and turntable neck. | Retain the focused SNOT head lesson, linked into the whole package; consolidate duplicate geometry. |
| Cat whole | Four legs, separate white paws, curved torso, raised tail and an oversized face are visible. | Retain compact articulated animal composition. Do not describe all of it as fixed SNOT. |
| Cat head | Flat face is posed upright by a ball neck. Brow bracket side studs are unused. Ear strips turn about a single engaged stud. | Keep those facts in the retrieval excerpt and adaptation guidance; do not imply a bracket-fixed vertical face. |
| Cat leg | Hip socket, shin and ankle clip form the three-piece section; the paws belong to the parent. | Retain a narrow articulation lesson, explicitly excluding paws; consolidate duplicate source. |
| Halcyon whole | Long nose, twin pods, thin wings, curved skins, paired intakes/exhausts, striping and stand match the source. | Retain as one original core-and-skin study. Remove the guide and tooling preference making it the default for every spaceship. |
| Halcyon hull and skins | Exposed course and isolated panels show upright local constructions rotated onto sideways stud faces. Nose, strakes and wing splices interleave with the keel. | Preserve the attachment calculations and actual handed layouts. Root reading order must not be advertised as a physical build sequence. |
| Halcyon cockpit | The named cockpit section is a single transparent canopy; floor is in the hull. | Label the lesson canopy placement. Do not retrieve it as a furnished cockpit assembly. |
| Halcyon stand | Round column and broad plate support the central underside visually. | Keep as part of this display model, with balance and clutch strength unproven. |

Every section has an individual observation and its images in [snot-audit.json](snot-audit.json). Parrot and cat source credits remain mandatory; do not invent licensing where none is declared. Existing scoped diagnostics remain authoritative: optimistic connection groups and uncovered collision shapes are not physical certification.

The generated inspection images are framing aids only; independent panel scaling must not be used to compare dimensions. [Image hashes](../../../../output/kb-visual-audit/snot-inspected-image-hashes.json) pin the views opened.
