# Sopwith Camel display biplane: SNOT fuselage, planked wings and rigging

The Sopwith Camel display biplane: a fuselage of log bricks, hinge-plate side walls and SNOT panels around a latticed windscreen; two planked wings of 1×3 tile rows on plate spars joined by suspension-arm struts; a radial engine face and geared propeller; a wedge-plate tailplane and striped rudder on handles; towball-hinged ailerons and a shock-absorber landing gear; strings as rigging and rubber bands as tyres. Measured: span 59 studs, length 50, height 23, 811 parts plus string and band segments, 8% Technic. Use it for the proportions and module split of a display biplane: span 1.2× length, upper wing above the fuselage top, lower wing at the belly, rudder and ailerons as separate hinged sections. Inspiration only: the string and band sections are LDCad segments; the detail packages from this source (propeller, tailplane, aileron, rudder) are reusable on their own.

Read [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md) for the construction, what the parent supplies, how to read the board and the limits; [index.html](index.html) holds the completed-model views. Source: `10226-1.mpd` / `10226 - main.ldr` (root section). Style: display SNOT biplane. Atlas: [examples/aircraft-atlas](../aircraft-atlas/README.md).
