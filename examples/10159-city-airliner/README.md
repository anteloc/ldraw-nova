# City airliner from moulded wing, tail and wedge plates

The classic City airliner in one studs-up section: a 4-wide brick fuselage with window bricks under an openable double-slope roof, moulded 7×12 wing plates, a T-tail with the printed 4867 tailplane on a brick fin, two 4868a/4869 engine pods on the rear fuselage sides, a wedge-plate nose with a 2437 windscreen, and wheel-plate landing gear. Measured: span 25 studs, length 29, height 14, 121 parts, no Technic. Use it for the proportions of a small City airliner: span about equal to length, rear engines, T-tail, a 4-wide cabin with one window per two studs. Inspiration only: the embedded printed tailplane lacks a part classification; use the library 4867 pattern part when rebuilding.

Read [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md) for the construction, what the parent supplies, how to read the board and the limits; [index.html](index.html) holds the completed-model views. Source: `10159-1.mpd` / `10159 - airplane.ldr` (placed by `10159 - main.ldr`). Style: classic City studs-up. Atlas: [examples/aircraft-atlas](../aircraft-atlas/README.md).
