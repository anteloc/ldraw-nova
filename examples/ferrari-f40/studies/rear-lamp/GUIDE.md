# Rear lamp and grille on perpendicular faces

**Lesson.** One small bracket-based corner can support two different visible surfaces without a bulky brick core.

**Construction.** Four source stages build an eight-piece corner from plate supports, a 99207 bracket, a jumper, a dish lamp and two grille tiles.

**Interfaces.** Read both views: the dish and grille occupy perpendicular planes. The underside support and exposed studs must meet the rear clamshell; a rendered lamp does not establish those parent connections.

**Parent context.** subModel-52.ldr declares two placements in its step 10 at [-14,-25,-123] and [-14,-25,137], with their source rotations. The matching rear body area appears in main source step 50. Preserve the distinction between the saved clamshell and the flattened car.

**Reuse notes.** Useful for vehicle lights beside vents, or compact corners with two surface treatments. Reinspect the dish and jumper offsets when changing either part. Keep all original colours unless deliberately redesigning the detail.
