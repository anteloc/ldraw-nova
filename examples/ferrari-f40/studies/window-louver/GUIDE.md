# Repeated sideways rear-window louver

**Lesson.** Make one small louver module, then place repeats on explicit stepped mounting planes.

**Construction.** Three source stages combine a 3666 rail, two 30413 translucent panels and two 87087 side-stud bricks. The module contains five parts.

**Interfaces.** The side-stud bricks turn the panel faces perpendicular to the rail. The surrounding clamshell supplies the repeated mounts; the local origin is not an automatically verified connector.

**Parent context.** subModel-52.ldr places three copies in step 15 at [-90,-75,7], [-130,-83,7] and [-170,-91,7], using its declared rotations. Their origins advance 40 LDU longitudinally and 8 LDU vertically in that parent frame.

**Reuse notes.** Transfer the repeated-module approach to vents, glazing or grille blades. The 40-by-8 progression belongs to this assembly, not to every louver design. Preserve joint seating and inspect clearance after changing the pitch.
