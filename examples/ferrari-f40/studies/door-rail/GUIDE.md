# Repeated brackets behind a narrow door rail

**Lesson.** A thin visible trim rail can also carry a repeated sideways stud field behind it.

**Construction.** Two source stages combine a 6636 tile, two small spacers/caps and three 44728 brackets into a six-piece rail.

**Interfaces.** The brackets expose a common perpendicular stud face. Their parent door supplies the remaining panel and hinge stack; the rail alone is not a complete opening door.

**Parent context.** subModel-34.ldr declares this child at [0,-88,0], in source step 7. The complete car's corresponding door is added in main source step 43. Its saved door is a separate callout, not a declared child of main.ldr.

**Reuse notes.** Transfer the repeated-bracket pattern to door trim or another narrow fascia. Keep the original spacing until actual sockets on the new panel have been inspected, then check the combined assembly.
