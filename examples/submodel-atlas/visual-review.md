# Visual review

Every entry was reviewed after its final render. The reviewer opened each board image directly and every step page plus `renders/home.png` through contact sheets built from those exact files (`./ldraw-xray mosaic`, 4 columns, 400 px panels). No single page was needed at full size; open them for small connections. Each folder's `visual-review.json` lists every image and binds the review to the current `manual.json` by hash; any re-render deletes it.

The candidates themselves were chosen and reviewed by the user from earlier inspection boards. These reviews check the promoted build pages and boards against the written notes.
