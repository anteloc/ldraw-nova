# Cathedral glazing fittings

Two standalone glazing fits are retained from the cathedral study. Their source bytes are unchanged. The complete cathedral is [archived](../archive/cathedral/README.md); these fittings do not admit that scene or establish a new host interface.

## Cathedral window

Isolated fit fixture contains one repeated pointed-arch bay. Two rectangular glazed windows sit below a large OPEN upper arch; do not describe fully glazed Gothic window. White paired arches, side posts and upper fill surround two tan framed printed panes; actual fixture and four cathedral wall contexts checked. Consolidate as one six-stud bay lesson.

Static visual evidence only unless the linked package records specific further checks. Search eligibility does not certify fit, motion or strength.

## Cathedral rose

Rose motif plus rear side-stud support brick; supports a narrow mounting lesson, not a whole cathedral endorsement. Transparent blue round backing, white radial dish and transparent amber centre tile; appears above west-front entrance. Decorative applied motif, not a demonstrated through-wall opening.

Static visual evidence only unless the linked package records specific further checks. Search eligibility does not certify fit, motion or strength.
