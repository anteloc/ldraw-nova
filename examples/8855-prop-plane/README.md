# Studded Technic floatplane with articulated wings and a piston engine

The 1988 studded Technic floatplane: a Technic-brick fuselage with an open cockpit and pilot, a high wing of Technic-brick spars and wing-brick ribs on universal joints, a piston engine behind a three-blade propeller, a tail boom with a 7×12 wing-plate tailplane and jumper-plate fin, and twin floats on an axle truss with wheels inside. Measured: span 72 studs, length 50, height 26, 598 parts, 66% Technic. Use it for the proportions and module layout of a studded Technic floatplane: span 1.4× length, high wing, floats two-thirds of the length. Inspiration only: the author scaled or skewed eight placements to match the strained real model, so rebuild rather than export.

Read [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md) for the construction, what the parent supplies, how to read the board and the limits; [index.html](index.html) holds the completed-model views. Source: `8855-1.mpd` / `8855 - Prop Plane.ldr` (root section). Style: studded Technic (1988). Atlas: [examples/aircraft-atlas](../aircraft-atlas/README.md).
