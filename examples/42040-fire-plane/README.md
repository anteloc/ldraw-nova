# Technic fire plane: studless liftarm airframe with panels and a geared tail

A studless Technic fire plane: three red spar beams carry panel wings, a liftarm fin and a joystick-geared stabiliser; a two-cylinder engine with a three-blade rotor sits in the nose; V-strut landing gear on treaded 61254 tyres hangs under a liftarm belly; the water bay door works on a rubber band. Measured: span 53 studs, length 42, height 23, 570 parts, 94% Technic. Use it for the layout of a studless Technic prop plane: a floor-and-spar frame, panels as wing and tail skins, and one cockpit lever driving a surface. Inspiration only: the embedded tyres and the rubber band are raw geometry; use the library 61254 and the flex workflow for the band when rebuilding.

Read [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md) for the construction, what the parent supplies, how to read the board and the limits; [index.html](index.html) holds the completed-model views. Source: `42040-1.mpd` / `42040 - Fire Plane.ldr` (root section). Style: studless Technic. Atlas: [examples/aircraft-atlas](../aircraft-atlas/README.md).
