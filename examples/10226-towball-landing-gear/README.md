# Biplane landing gear: suspension-arm V struts and shock absorbers on an open beam frame, pulley-and-dish wheels

Biplane landing gear from Technic parts: a 7×5 open-centre beam frame pinned under the fuselage, two V struts of 1×9 suspension arms, two 6.5L shock absorbers as the sprung legs, and a 12-long axle through the V apexes carrying two 5D pulley wheels capped with 4×4 inverted dishes as the wheels. Rebuild the technique rather than copying: a pinned beam frame as the gear mount, suspension arms as struts, shocks as legs, and pulley-plus-dish wheels for a vintage look. The shock's spring is drawn as raw mesh geometry in the source, which is why this is an inspiration entry.

Read [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md) for the construction, what the parent supplies, how to read the board and the limits; [index.html](index.html) holds the build pages. Source: `10226-1.mpd` / `10226 - step58.ldr` (placed by `10226 - main.ldr`). Style: display SNOT biplane. Atlas: [examples/aircraft-atlas](../aircraft-atlas/README.md).
